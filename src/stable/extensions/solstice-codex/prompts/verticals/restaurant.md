# Vertical Template Pack: Restaurant

Use for restaurants, chef concepts, bistros, bakeries, wine bars, and reservation-led hospitality.

## Content Structure

1. Hero with cuisine promise, neighborhood, service hours, reserve/order CTA, and a real signature dish.
2. Menu highlights with dietary markers, current price truth, and link to the complete menu.
3. Chef/story, sourcing philosophy, atmosphere gallery, press/reviews, and events/private dining.
4. Reservation flow with party size, date, cancellation policy, accessibility, parking, and contact.
5. Location, opening hours, delivery/takeaway truth, newsletter, and mobile sticky reserve bar.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `ערב שנבנה סביב חומרי גלם, קצב ואירוח.`
- CTAs: `מזמינים שולחן` · `פותחים תפריט`.
- Write sensorially but specifically; never invent awards, suppliers, dishes, or availability.

## Image Direction

- Real dishes in service light, hands at work, room atmosphere, chef portrait, ingredient details.
- Avoid empty plate grids and generic overhead stock. Food color must remain natural.

## Live References

- https://noma.dk/ — seasonal storytelling, journal, and experience framing.
- https://www.disfrutarbarcelona.com/ — menu hierarchy, team credibility, and practical booking detail.

## Motion Fit

Editorial chapter transitions, menu reveals, and ambient video can be cinematic; reservation controls must never move away.
