# Vertical Template Pack: Portfolio

Use for designers, photographers, artists, filmmakers, architects, and independent creative studios.

## Content Structure

1. Identity-led hero with discipline, point of view, availability, and selected-work entry.
2. Curated projects, not a dump: role, context, constraint, process, result, media, and credits.
3. About/capabilities, client or publication proof, services, recognition, and downloadable profile if useful.
4. Contact path with project-fit questions, location/timezone, social links, and clear availability.
5. Accessible project navigation, image credits, performance-conscious galleries, and strong mobile sequencing.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `עבודה שנבנתה סביב רעיון, לא סביב תבנית.`
- CTAs: `נכנסים לפרויקטים` · `מדברים על הפרויקט הבא`.
- Use first-person clarity and concrete roles; credit collaborators and never inflate scope.

## Image Direction

- One coherent visual system per project, full-bleed hero, process details, prototypes, and outcomes.
- Art direction may vary by case study, but typography and navigation stay recognizably yours.

## Motion Fit

Portfolio motion can be expressive when it reveals authorship. Always support keyboard, reduced motion, and direct project URLs.
