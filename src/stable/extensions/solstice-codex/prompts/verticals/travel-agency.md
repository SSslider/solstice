# Vertical Template Pack: Travel Agency

Use for travel agencies, tour operators, private guides, destination specialists, and curated group travel.

## Content Structure

1. Hero with travel style, destination promise, next departure or planning CTA, and real destination media.
2. Trip discovery by destination, season, duration, pace, budget band, and traveler type.
3. Trip detail: itinerary by day, inclusions/exclusions, accommodation, guide, group size, price, availability.
4. Why travel with us, guide/team profiles, safety/support, sourced traveler stories, and FAQ.
5. Consultation or booking flow, cancellation/payment terms, practical preparation, and sticky mobile inquiry.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `לא עוד רשימת יעדים. מסע שתפור לקצב שלכם.`
- CTAs: `מתכננים מסלול` · `רואים יציאות קרובות`.
- Be vivid about experience and exact about logistics; never invent availability, visa rules, or prices.

## Image Direction

- Destination-wide scene, human-scale moments, local texture, guide portraits, route/map details, accommodation truth.
- Avoid tourist-collage stock. Each trip needs one coherent destination story and proper rights/credits.
