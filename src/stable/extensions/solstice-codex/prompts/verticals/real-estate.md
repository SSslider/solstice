# Vertical Template Pack: Real Estate

Use for brokers, boutique agencies, developers, property marketers, and neighborhood specialists.

## Content Structure

1. Hero search or curated collection with area, property type, intent, and contact CTA.
2. Filterable listings with verified status, price truth, key facts, map context, and no fake scarcity.
3. Property detail story: gallery, floor plan, amenities, neighborhood, agent, viewing request.
4. Seller and buyer journeys, valuation lead form, agent bios, transactions, and sourced testimonials.
5. Area guides, market insight, legal caveats, office/contact, and sticky mobile inquiry.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `נכסים מדויקים. החלטות עם הקשר.`
- CTAs: `מחפשים נכס` · `מתאמים הערכת שווי`.
- State listing availability and prices as dated facts; do not imply exclusivity without proof.

## Image Direction

- Architectural wide shots, honest daylight interiors, floor-plan crops, neighborhood life, agent portraits.
- Keep geometry straight and disclose renders. Never mix unrelated stock properties into a listing.
