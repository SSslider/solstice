# Vertical Template Pack: Events

Use for event planners, producers, wedding studios, conferences, brand activations, and private celebrations.

## Content Structure

1. Hero naming event type, signature value, service region, inquiry CTA, and one real event sequence.
2. Services by scale and need: strategy, concept, venue, production, vendors, guest journey, on-site management.
3. Case studies with brief, constraints, concept, execution, gallery/video, credits, and sourced result.
4. Process, team, trusted partners, press/reviews, availability windows, and realistic budget guidance.
5. Qualified inquiry form, FAQ, response time, cancellation boundaries, and mobile contact CTA.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `מהרעיון הראשון ועד הרגע שבו האורחים נכנסים.`
- CTAs: `מתחילים לתכנן` · `רואים אירועים שבנינו`.
- Be emotional in the story and exact in logistics; credit venues, photographers, and vendors.

## Image Direction

- Full event arc: empty space, setup, detail, arrival, peak moment, people, and close. Use one photographer set per story.
- Avoid mixed weddings from unrelated sources. Rights, credits, and consent are mandatory.
