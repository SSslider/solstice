# Vertical Template Pack: eCommerce

Use for direct-to-consumer shops, catalogs, Shopify stores, and online retail brands.

## Content Structure

1. Hero merchandising one current collection with primary shop CTA and shipping/returns truth.
2. Category navigation, product discovery, filters, search, badges, and collection storytelling.
3. Product detail: media, variants, stock, price, delivery estimate, size/specs, reviews, and add-to-cart.
4. Value proof, comparison, bundles, UGC, brand story, support, returns, and trust/payment signals.
5. Cart and checkout handoff, lifecycle capture, mobile sticky add-to-cart, and accessible empty/error states.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `נבחר בקפידה. מגיע בלי הפתעות.`
- CTAs: `לקולקציה` · `מוסיפים לסל`.
- Use specific materials, fit, dimensions, and delivery facts; never invent stock, discounts, or reviews.

## Image Direction

- Consistent product packshots, scale/context image, material macro, real-use lifestyle, and short demo clips.
- Preserve color accuracy and alt text; do not use generated imagery as product evidence.

## Live References

- https://www.allbirds.com/ — material proof, guided category discovery, and product education.
- https://www.gymshark.com/ — campaign merchandising, collection hierarchy, and mobile commerce.
- https://www.nike.com/ — search/filter patterns, product media, and member benefits.

## Motion Fit

Use lightweight product transitions and collection reveals. Performance, variant selection, and add-to-cart outrank spectacle.
