# Vertical Template Pack: Beauty / Cosmetics

Use for cosmeticians, skincare studios, aesthetic clinics, nail studios, and treatment-led beauty brands.

## Content Structure

1. Hero with signature treatment, audience, location, booking CTA, and one sourced proof.
2. Concern-led treatment finder: acne, pigmentation, texture, anti-aging, lashes, brows, nails.
3. Treatment menu with duration, downtime, price anchor, contraindication handoff, and expected process.
4. Practitioner credentials, hygiene standards, consultation flow, and real studio imagery.
5. Consent-based results, verified reviews, product routines, FAQ, booking policy, and sticky mobile CTA.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `טיפול שמתחיל באבחון, לא בהבטחה.`
- CTAs: `מתאימים טיפול` · `קובעים ייעוץ`.
- Use precise, warm language; never guarantee medical or cosmetic outcomes.

## Image Direction

- Macro textures, real hands and tools, natural skin, daylight studio portraits, disciplined ingredient still life.
- Avoid over-retouching, generic spa stones, and synthetic before/after comparisons.

## Live References

- https://www.glossier.com/ — product storytelling, community proof, and tactile art direction.
- https://www.aesop.com/ — restrained editorial system and ingredient-led hierarchy.
- https://www.drsturm.com/ — treatment education and premium clinical merchandising.

## Motion Fit

Use ingredient layers, texture wipes, and slow treatment-story reveals. Booking and safety copy remain stable.
