# Vertical Template Pack: Renovation / Services

Use for renovation contractors, carpenters, repair specialists, kitchen/bath studios, and home-improvement teams.

## Content Structure

1. Hero with exact service, service area, availability/response truth, estimate CTA, and a real finished project.
2. Services by homeowner problem, typical scope, starting range or estimate process, and what is excluded.
3. Project case studies with before, plan, build, after, timeline, materials, constraints, and client permission.
4. Process: site visit, quote, contract, schedule, protection, milestones, change orders, handover, warranty.
5. Credentials/insurance truth, team, sourced reviews, FAQ, service map, emergency boundary, and sticky call/WhatsApp.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `שיפוץ עם תוכנית ברורה, אתר מסודר ועדכונים בדרך.`
- CTAs: `מבקשים הערכת פרויקט` · `שולחים תמונות ב־WhatsApp`.
- Avoid “best price” and impossible timelines. State licensing, warranty, and estimates only when verified.

## Image Direction

- Matched before/after angles, real team at work, material samples, hidden-detail proof, and clean finished rooms.
- Label renders and avoid unrelated luxury stock. Show process quality, not only polished outcomes.
