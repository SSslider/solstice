# Vertical Template Pack: Barber / Hair Salon

Use for barbers, hair salons, tattoo studios, and appointment-led hair businesses. Cosmetics and treatment-led beauty use the dedicated beauty pack.

## Hebrew Copy

- Taste and speed: show the actual style, services, price anchors, location, and instant booking/WhatsApp.
- Content tone: local, confident, visual Hebrew. Short copy beats paragraphs.
- Suggested lead: `דיוק בידיים. אופי בכל תספורת.` CTAs: `קובעים תור` · `רואים עבודות`.
- Visual language: real work gallery, strong before/after media, clean menu, social proof from Instagram/TikTok.

## Content Structure

1. Hero with first-viewport gallery/video still, booking CTA, city/neighborhood, and opening hours.
2. Services and prices/menu: haircuts, beard, color, nails, treatments, packages.
3. Work gallery with categories and real assets; no stock-looking filler.
4. Staff/artists with specialties and availability.
5. Booking flow: choose service, pick time, WhatsApp/call, cancellation note.
6. Social proof: reviews, Instagram feed links, tagged work, client quotes.
7. Location block: parking/transit, map, accessibility, walk-in policy.
8. Mobile sticky booking bar.

## Data To Ask Or Infer

- Services, prices, booking link, WhatsApp, Instagram, address, hours, staff, brand colors, style references.
- If assets are missing, generate branded photographic assets and clearly label them as generated placeholders for replacement.

## Image Direction

- Real cuts from consistent angles, hands/tools in action, shop atmosphere, staff portraits, and texture closeups.
- Keep before/after truthful and consented; avoid generic fashion editorials detached from actual work.

## Live References

- https://www.murdocklondon.com/ — service/product architecture and strong house identity.
- https://www.ruffians.co.uk/ — location-led booking and editorial grooming content.
- https://www.fellowbarber.com/ — direct appointment conversion and work-focused imagery.

## Motion Fit

- Stronger motion is acceptable: horizontal galleries, masked image reveals, marquee service strip, scroll-linked before/after.
- Keep booking controls stable and fast; motion must not block conversion.
