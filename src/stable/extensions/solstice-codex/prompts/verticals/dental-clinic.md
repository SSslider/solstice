# Vertical Template Pack: Dental Clinic

Use for dentists, orthodontists, oral surgeons, hygienists, and multidisciplinary dental clinics in Israel.

## Content Structure

1. Hero: specialty, city, appointment CTA, phone/WhatsApp, and one verified trust proof.
2. Treatment paths by patient need: prevention, restoration, implants, orthodontics, aesthetics, urgent care.
3. Dentist/team profiles with licenses, specialties, languages, and real portraits.
4. Patient journey: assessment, imaging, treatment plan, finance/insurance, treatment, follow-up.
5. Ethical case gallery using consented, sourced media only; never invent outcomes.
6. Reviews with source labels, FAQ, accessibility, parking, emergency instructions, and sticky mobile booking.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `טיפול מדויק, הסבר בגובה העיניים, ותוכנית שרואים מראש.`
- Primary CTA: `קובעים בדיקה` · Secondary: `שולחים הודעה ב־WhatsApp`.
- Tone is calm and concrete. Avoid painless guarantees, fear language, or fabricated medical claims.

## Image Direction

- Real clinic, dentist-patient consultation, equipment, and team; clean whites with a warm human accent.
- Prefer wide environmental portraits and close detail over stock smiles. Before/after requires written consent.

## Live References

- https://www.tend.com/ — plain-language treatment navigation and approachable conversion.
- https://www.dntlbar.com/ — service merchandising and contemporary clinic identity.
- https://www.dentalhouse.com.au/ — team credibility, patient journey, and educational content.

## Motion Fit

Subtle service reveals and a treatment timeline are enough. Keep booking controls fixed, accessible, and usable with reduced motion.
