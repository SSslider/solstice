# Vertical Template Pack: Medical Clinic

Use for doctors, physiotherapy, diagnostics, and private healthcare practices in the Israeli market. Dental and cosmetic clinics use their dedicated packs.

## Hebrew Copy

- Trust first: visible doctor/team credibility, licenses, years of experience, and clinic location.
- Conversion path: book appointment, WhatsApp, call, navigation, and emergency/urgent CTA when relevant.
- Content tone: calm, precise, professional Hebrew. Avoid exaggerated promises or medical guarantees.
- Suggested lead: `רפואה פרטית עם זמן להסביר ותוכנית שאפשר להבין.` CTAs: `קובעים תור` · `מדברים עם המרפאה`.
- Visual language: clean clinical whites balanced with one warm accent, real clinic/team/treatment imagery, clear accessibility.

## Content Structure

1. Hero with specialty, location, appointment CTA, phone/WhatsApp, and one concrete trust proof.
2. Treatment/service grid grouped by patient need, not internal jargon.
3. Doctor/team profile with credentials, languages, and clinic photos.
4. Patient journey: consultation, diagnosis, treatment plan, follow-up.
5. Before/after or case evidence only when legal/ethical and with real assets.
6. Reviews/testimonials with source labels; never fabricate names.
7. FAQ for cost, insurance, duration, pain/recovery, parking, accessibility.
8. Sticky mobile action bar with call, WhatsApp, and directions.

## Data To Ask Or Infer

- Specialty, city/neighborhood, lead doctor, services, accepted insurance/private only, phone/WhatsApp, Google Maps link, opening hours.
- Regulatory caveat: if claims are medical, keep them conservative and suggest consultation rather than certainty.

## Image Direction

- Real practitioners, consultation, clinic environment, diagnostic context, and accessibility details.
- Avoid generic white-coat stock and any visual that implies an unsupported outcome.

## Live References

- https://www.onemedical.com/ — patient-first navigation and practical access information.
- https://www.forward.com/ — clear care model explanation and productized patient journey.
- https://www.mayoclinic.org/ — trustworthy condition/service information architecture.

## Motion Fit

- Subtle trust-building motion: service cards reveal, clinic photo parallax, process timeline, sticky appointment CTA.
- Avoid flashy motion that makes healthcare feel unserious.
