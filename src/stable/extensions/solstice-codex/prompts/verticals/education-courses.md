# Vertical Template Pack: Education / Courses

Use for academies, schools, online courses, cohorts, workshops, and expert-led learning businesses.

## Content Structure

1. Hero with learner, outcome, format, next start date or on-demand truth, and enroll/syllabus CTA.
2. Curriculum by module, learning outcomes, prerequisites, workload, delivery format, and assessment.
3. Instructor credibility, sample lesson, student work, sourced outcomes, community/support, and certification truth.
4. Plans, financing, cohort schedule, FAQ, refund policy, accessibility, and employer/team options.
5. Enrollment journey with transparent steps, onboarding expectations, and mobile sticky enrollment CTA.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `לומדים דרך עשייה, יוצאים עם יכולת שאפשר להראות.`
- CTAs: `פותחים סילבוס` · `נרשמים למחזור הבא`.
- Promise skills and evidence, not guaranteed jobs or income. Label outcome data and sample work sources.

## Image Direction

- Real instructor, learner work, classroom/session, platform UI, annotated projects, and community moments.
- Avoid graduation-cap stock and fake certificates. Show the learning experience itself.

## Live References

- https://www.masterclass.com/ — instructor-led merchandising and strong lesson previews.
- https://www.coursera.org/ — catalog discovery, credentials, and outcome framing.
- https://www.duolingo.com/ — progressive learning narrative and approachable product demonstration.

## Motion Fit

Curriculum progress, lesson previews, and interactive exercises can animate; price, schedule, and enrollment stay legible.
