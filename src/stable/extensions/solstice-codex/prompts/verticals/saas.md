# Vertical Template Pack: SaaS

Use for B2B/B2C software, developer tools, AI products, and subscription platforms.

## Content Structure

1. Hero naming user, job, outcome, product proof, and one primary trial/demo CTA.
2. Interactive or recorded product workflow; show the UI before broad benefit prose.
3. Use cases by role, feature-to-outcome sections, integrations, security, migration, and reliability.
4. Plans with billing truth, limits, comparison, FAQ, case studies, docs, and status/support paths.
5. Final CTA matched to funnel stage plus accessible legal, privacy, and cookie controls.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `פחות תיאום ידני. יותר עבודה שמתקדמת לבד.`
- CTAs: `מתחילים ניסיון` · `רואים דמו`.
- Tie every claim to a visible workflow or sourced metric; avoid “revolutionary” filler.

## Image Direction

- Crisp real UI crops, annotated workflows, integration diagrams, role-specific outcomes, human context sparingly.
- Never show fictional dashboards as shipped product truth; label concepts and prototypes.

Product demos, cursor choreography, and workflow diagrams can carry motion; pricing and accessibility remain static and testable.
