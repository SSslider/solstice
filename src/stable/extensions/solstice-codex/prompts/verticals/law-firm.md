# Vertical Template Pack: Law Firm

Use for lawyers, boutique law firms, notaries, mediation, and Israeli legal service providers.

## Positioning

- Authority and clarity: practice areas, attorney credentials, representative cases, and clear next step.
- Content tone: confident, discreet, practical Hebrew. No guaranteed legal outcomes.
- Visual language: restrained premium, strong typography, high contrast, office/court/document imagery, no generic scales-of-justice cliches unless requested.

## Required Sections

1. Hero with practice niche, jurisdiction/context, consultation CTA, and direct contact.
2. Practice areas grouped by client problem: business, real estate, family, labor, litigation, contracts, etc.
3. Attorney bios with bar admission, education, languages, notable experience.
4. Process: initial call, document review, strategy, representation, follow-up.
5. Case studies or anonymized scenarios with outcome caveats.
6. Trust signals: memberships, publications, media, client sectors, response time.
7. FAQ for fees, first consultation, documents needed, timelines, confidentiality.
8. Contact section with office map, hours, WhatsApp/call, and secure form.

## Data To Ask Or Infer

- Practice areas, city, attorney names, license/profile links, consultation model, urgent cases handled, languages.
- Compliance caveat: never imply guaranteed wins; phrase results as experience or examples.

## Motion Fit

- Editorial motion: sticky table-of-contents, case cards, document-layer parallax, measured section reveals.
- Keep motion quiet; the site should feel serious and repeatedly usable.
