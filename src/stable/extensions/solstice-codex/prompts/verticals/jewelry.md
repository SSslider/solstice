# Vertical Template Pack: Jewelry

Use for fine jewelry, independent jewelers, diamond studios, custom pieces, and premium accessories.

## Content Structure

1. Hero with signature collection, craft/value promise, shop or consultation CTA, and real product macro.
2. Shop by category, collection, occasion, material, stone, and price band with strong filtering.
3. Product detail: multi-angle media, scale, metal/stone specs, sizing, care, provenance, price, and delivery.
4. Craft story, custom process, certification, repair/service, gifting, reviews, and appointment booking.
5. Shipping/returns/warranty truth, secure payment, care guide, and mobile sticky purchase/consultation CTA.

## Hebrew Copy

- Lead: `חומר, אור ועבודת יד שנשארים.`
- CTAs: `מגלים את הקולקציה` · `קובעים פגישה אישית`.
- State carat, material, origin, sizing, and availability precisely; no invented rarity or certification.

## Image Direction

- High-resolution macro, skin-scale context, side/back/clasp angles, craft bench, packaging, and controlled reflections.
- Keep metal and gemstone colors accurate. Generated imagery is mood only, never product proof.

## Live References

- https://www.mejuri.com/ — everyday-category discovery and product education.
- https://www.tiffany.com/ — heritage storytelling, service paths, and premium product detail.
- https://www.monicavinader.com/ — personalization, gifting, and layered collection navigation.

## Motion Fit

Slow macro reveals, light sweeps, and collection transitions support luxury; product specs and purchase controls stay still.
