# Site Brief Approval Contract

For a new website, stop before application-source edits and present one concise approval page containing:

- audience and primary action;
- ordered page sections;
- visual tone and copy direction;
- 3–5 live vertical references to research;
- the classified motion level: static, subtle, cinematic or explicit ScrollWorld.

The operator may edit every field. Do not start implementation until the approval action emits `[FELIX_APPROVED_SITE_BRIEF]`. After approval, treat the brief as the build contract. Research the named/reference direction first, then design and code. If later evidence requires a material direction change, surface it instead of silently replacing the approved brief.
